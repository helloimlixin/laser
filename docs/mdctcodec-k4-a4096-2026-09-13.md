The current comparison uses **LASER: one shared 4096-atom dictionary, K4**, and
**RVQ: four independent 1024-entry codebooks, depth four**. Both use 32-dimensional
vectors, so both dictionaries contain 4096 vectors and 131072 learned scalar
values. Preparation verifies this against the actual RVQ initialization tensors.
RVQ's additional input/output projections and LASER's coefficient levels and
dictionary-learning procedure remain differences. Matching dictionary capacity
does not imply equal total parameters, transmitted vocabularies, or bitrate.

This supersedes the short K4/A1024 run `nkhmncew`. Its checkpoints and logs are
retained. The A4096 run starts afresh from the exact encoder, decoder and
discriminator initialization used by the completed matched RVQ control, rather
than carrying over the smaller-dictionary model's training history.

Both use the same 150Hz latent rate, 48kHz VCTK waveform inputs, 40936 training
utterances, seed, 48-example batches, 7960-sample crops, audio losses, optimizer,
and 200000 generator/discriminator update budgets. Each complete production
epoch checks its crop/data digest against the recorded RVQ control. An
intentional partial-epoch stop preserves its checkpoint and does not claim that
partial epoch matches a complete reference epoch.

LASER retains OMP, the alternating residual dictionary update, and eight signed
nonzero coefficient centers plus zero. Initial centers use 256 training crops;
training-only Lloyd EMA updates run after each complete optimizer pair. All
validation waveforms are decoded from actual entropy payloads using checkpointed
dictionary, coefficient levels and Huffman tables, with no validation fitting.

## Rate and validation

The joint sparse vocabulary is `1 + 4096 * 8 = 32769` per selection. Four
fixed-width 16-bit joint integers at 150Hz cost **9.6kbps**, before headers.
Huffman coding uses training-only per-depth frequencies and temporal-repeat
symbols; all coefficient information, the 12-byte header/checksum, and final
padding count toward measured payload rate. **6kbps is still a target, not an
achieved result.** The current training objective has no explicit rate penalty.
A fair 6kbps performance claim requires measured bitrate to meet the budget;
final rate-distortion reporting should also entropy-code the RVQ control.

Validation uses the fixed 128 full recordings and selects checkpoints by ViSQOL
audio48k. Latest plus the best three checkpoints upload to W&B every five
completed epochs and at successful exit. Eight predetermined examples include
sample audio, waveforms, MDCT, log-mel and STFT-error plots at that cadence.
The run targets 200k updates with a 12 assigned-GPU-hour ceiling.
