The objective now gives probability credit to complete reconstructions:

    L(z,H) = -log sum_{S in unique valid candidates}
                    p_theta(S | H) exp(-||v(S)-z||^2 / tau)

Here `z` is the full continuous cached sparse vector, `v(S)` is the complete candidate's reconstructed vector, and every candidate is evaluated under its own causal atom/coefficient prefix and the same spatial context `H`. The model still uses the valid factorization `p(a,c|h)=p(a|h)p(c|a,h)`. All head probabilities use the full vocabulary. Exact duplicate sequences are counted once; distinct encodings of equivalent vectors retain their probability mass. There is no division by candidate count. A fixed factor `1/(2*4)` keeps the optimizer's loss scale comparable to the old equal-head average.

Each site includes all 24 permutations of its original complete atom/physical-coefficient pairs and four complete stochastic proposals. A permutation keeps each physical coefficient paired with its atom, rounds to the existing destination-depth bins, and rejects out-of-range values. The candidates are deduplicated, leaving about 20 valid sequences per site on the checked Church rows. Permutations broaden ordering coverage of the same support set; the four stochastic proposals provide limited exploration of other support sets. This is a finite-set lower bound on full vector-kernel probability, not an exhaustive likelihood over all sparse representations. The earlier proposal's anchoring and limited support-set coverage are not claimed solved globally.

Spatial inputs are the summed physical vectors of completed sites. Depth inputs are physical partial sums. The learned atom/bin identity adapter and its coefficient-token embedding have been removed. The current-atom coefficient micro-transformer remains. Training and cached generation use the same physical inputs. Two equivalent physical contexts therefore produce identical spatial inputs, independent of their sparse encoding.

The stochastic context is the first draw from the previously calibrated complete-vector teacher, before candidate permutations or marginalization. This preserves the previous augmentation law, rather than sampling context from the alias-heavy likelihood posterior. The context temperature is 0.33584674003704434; the vector-kernel width is explicitly fixed to the same physical value. It is a score width, not a newly calibrated sampling temperature.
