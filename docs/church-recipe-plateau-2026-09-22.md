Through epoch 176, its best FID50k remains **9.95524 at epoch 57**, 119 epochs
earlier. Mean FID increased from 11.624 over epochs 61–80 to 12.198 over epochs
161–176; the latest score was 12.16068. Each evaluation uses 50,000 generated
images and the verified 126,227-image training reference with matching cache
preprocessing. The sampling settings and fixed evaluation seed are unchanged.

Both probes contain 300 images and use evaluation mode with fixed code histories.
The training probe remains part of the full optimization population; the validation
probe is the official validation split. Validation atom NLL reached its minimum at
epoch 19. By epoch 176, first-depth atom NLL was 0.304 on training images versus
15.108 on validation images. These diverging trends strongly support overfitting,
particularly for early sparse atom predictions. Atom NLL and coefficient KL have
different scales; their absolute values are not directly comparable.

This does not establish a unique cause of the FID plateau. The official validation
split is not an independently sampled holdout from the training LMDB, and FID is
computed against training images. The measured validation gap therefore diagnoses
generalization without replacing the user's requested FID selection criterion.
The fixed sampling seed also means epoch-to-epoch FID changes are not independent
sampling-seed replicates.

The learning rate remains on its requested cosine schedule, approximately 1.83e-4
at epoch 176, and logged gradients are finite. The investigation does not change
the active 300-epoch recipe. The epoch-57 best checkpoint and the latest full state
remain saved, with verified W&B uploads; the epoch-171 upload receipt already
includes the same best checkpoint in `selected-checkpoints:v45`.
