Its controller and workers were stopped at the step-1240 evaluation boundary; the full pending checkpoint is preserved and that interrupted evaluation is unscored. This document records the retired experiment, not the active recipe. The applicable results are the [sampling-only comparison](church-untruncated-sampling-2026-09-29.md).

This run replaces the prior RQ stage-2 architecture with a fresh model over the existing frozen LASER sparse codes. It retains the Church 256 dataset, dictionary, decoder, four depth scales and 2,048 coefficient bins. The previous best RQ checkpoint (FID 9.493208) and final 300-epoch checkpoint remain preserved and verified on W&B.

DCTransformer models sparse events with conditional categorical predictions, conditioning stacked decoders on an encoded partial representation and supplying local encoded context to the value decoder. Its decoder blocks use pre-normalization, ReZero residual gates and multiple feedforward layers per attention layer. These are the mechanisms transferred here. [Paper, sections 3.1–3.3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/nash21a/nash21a.pdf) and [supplement, appendix B](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/nash21a/nash21a-supp.pdf).

The implemented model has 336,167,227 parameters and 488 parameter tensors. Width 896 and 14 attention heads are shared across a four-block prefix encoder, a four-block support decoder, and a six-block value decoder. The feedforward-layer counts per block are [2,2,2,2],[2,2,2,4], and[2,2,2,2,2,7]. Separate input-field normalization prevents support history from overwhelming the selected atom or local reconstruction.

Each image contains 256 paired events. Four depth-major chunks each predict one full 64-site plane. The prefix encoder receives the physical latent sum from completed earlier planes only. Support and value decoders use causal self-attention and cross-attention into this frozen prefix. The value decoder additionally receives the selected current atom and encoded context gathered at its known spatial location. The decoder has 8 overlapping context positions plus the ordinary previous-event shift. Every overlap coefficient uses its original depth scale. Repeated atoms at one spatial site are masked identically during training and sampling.

Targets come from the original unclipped FP32 greedy-OMP cache, SHA256 4c03555ebedca1bf7b74f1db30e91752586889337a58547e856416c3aeeae586. Support IDs remain unchanged; coefficients map to their nearest existing bins, with ties resolved toward the lower index. The objective is 0.5×(support categorical NLL+conditional coefficient categorical NLL); the joint NLL sum is logged separately. There is no stochastic teacher, coefficient refit, re-encoding, reward, geometry penalty or contrastive objective.

Training starts from new random weights and an empty Adam state. It uses all eight H100 80GB GPUs, global batch 2,048, microbatch 128 and two accumulation steps. Every image is visited once per epoch, including the final 1,299-image batch with exact global weighting. The target is 300 epochs / 18,600 updates. LR warms up for 1,000 updates to 5e-4, then decays by cosine to zero. Adam betas (.9,.999), epsilon 1e-8 and zero weight decay are explicit default choices; gradient norm clipping is 1. Sampling uses full eligible support vocabulary and all coefficient bins at temperature 1 with nucleus filtering disabled. Generation uses BF16; decoder and official Inception evaluation use FP32 with TF32 disabled.

Samples are logged every 200 updates. Official 50k FID, including mean and covariance terms, runs every 620 updates and at the final boundary. Full local checkpoints are saved at sample/evaluation boundaries; full LAST/BEST are uploaded and verified at FID/final boundaries, avoiding the previous upload backlog. Pending evaluation, model/optimizer/scheduler, exact data cursor and all eight RNG streams are checkpointed. A supervisor permits up to three restarts from the latest complete or pending boundary. The first run checkpoint is saved before any optimizer update.

This is an adaptation, not a reproduction of the DCT benchmark. Spatial positions and sequence length are fixed, so there is no position predictor or EOS. OMP depth is not guaranteed frequency order. The physical prefix sums compress overcomplete support identities; they are not a dense DCT coefficient tensor. All four planes receive uniform supervision. The chunk/overlap sizes, field normalization, global image batch and 300-epoch budget are our choices. Exact exposure is 9,694,233,600 paired events; the source reports 300B target tokens for LSUN, with a different 384-long-side image protocol. Its 7.56 Church FID is not a promised result for this run. [Supplement, table 3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/nash21a/nash21a-supp.pdf).

Before launch, 13 model tests and seven codec/schedule tests passed. Independent checks verified causality and full/cached agreement with active ReZero gates, all 256 events and unequal physical depth scales. The frozen decoder matched the recovered decoder bit-for-bit on CPU. An eight-GPU, production-size preflight completed three full global-batch optimizer updates, exercised all 488 parameter gradients/states, generated valid images and passed FP32/BF16 cached-logit checks with 59 ReZero gates forced active. Steady preflight update time was 0.673 s, peak allocated memory 54.28 GiB/GPU. These are implementation checks, not evidence of converged image quality.
